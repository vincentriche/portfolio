Wanadev Experience

3D | WebGL engineer at Wanadev

After my graduation, I started a permanent contract at Wanadev, a French startup based in Lyon, France. Wanadev is specialized in development web, 3D WebGl and virtual reality.

I worked the WebGl team on the app Kazaplan. This tool allows sketching 2D plan of a house, a flat or any others constructions. You can switch in 3D mode to fly in the middles of the rooms, the floors and all the furniture you can add.
The power of kazaplan is that you can sketch and configure the house of your dreams right inside your web browser.
