Internship at LIRIS

This internship took place at Computer Science Laboratory in Image and Information Systems (LIRIS), a joint research unit supported in particular by the CNRS and University Lyon 1. Within the team GEOMOD under the tutelage of Mr. Adrien PEYTAVIE we realized, with a friend, a software for generate an atmosphere model by sketching or drawing.

This software can generate an exportable atmosphere model (in a rendering software) using one or more sketch (representing a type of cloud) created by the user from one or many camera points of view.
