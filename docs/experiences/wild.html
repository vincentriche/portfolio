Internship at Wild Sheep Studio

During my master's degree's final year, I did a six-month internship in a game studio. I had the chance to be hired in Wild Sheep Studio as a tool programmer.
This studio was created by a few people from Ubisoft Montpellier, and especially by Michel Ancel who created Rayman and Beyond Good&Evil.

During these months, I worked on WiLD, an exclusive PS4 game, produced by Sony Entertainment.
