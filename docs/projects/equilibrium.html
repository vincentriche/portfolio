Equilibrium

Equilibrium is a fun, physics based arcade game where you have to pile up cubes on your triangle. The more cubes you are holding, the quicker your score increases. Special cubes appear progressively during play, increasing the difficulty.

The game has been released on Android in June, 2015. It is still under development and updated regularly.
